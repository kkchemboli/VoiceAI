1. Company Overview

Expert Institute is an ISO 9001:2015 certified technical training institute specializing in electronics repair courses such as mobile, laptop, CCTV, and LED/LCD TV repairing. The institute provides practical chip-level training with real tools and devices to prepare students for jobs or starting their own repair businesses.

The institute has 15+ years of experience and has trained thousands of students who now work in service centers or run their own businesses.

2. Courses Offered

The institute provides multiple technical repair training programs.

Mobile Repairing Course

Covers smartphone troubleshooting and repair.

Includes Android, iPhone, GSM/CDMA devices.

Training includes hardware and software repair techniques.

iPhone Repairing Course

Chip-level repair training for Apple iPhones.

Includes motherboard repair, lens repair, and advanced diagnostics.

Laptop Repairing Course

Chip-level laptop motherboard repair.

Includes BIOS programming, CPU testing, and debugging.

LED / LCD Smart TV Repairing

TV motherboard diagnostics

Printer Repairing Course

Laserjet, Inkjet, and Dot Matrix printer repair training.

Courses are designed for beginners as well as experienced technicians.
