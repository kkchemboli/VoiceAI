Company Overview

Expert Institute of Advance Technologies Pvt. Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 certified technical training institute specializing in professional courses such as Mobile, iPhone, Laptop, MacBook, CCTV Camera, AC PCB and LED/Smart TV repair training.
The institute offers hands-on, chip-level training using real tools and live devices, enabling students to gain practical skills for employment or to start their own repair businesses.
With over 15 years of experience, the institute has successfully trained more than 1,00,000 students.
